  • UN Consolidated List (~25K records)
  • PEP & adverse media detection

6 types of transaction anomaly detection automatically flag suspicious patterns including structuring, rapid movement, and geographic risk.

  • Structuring detection
  • Rapid movement analysis
  • Velocity anomalies
  • Geographic risk assessment

Automated FinCEN-compliant Suspicious Activity Reports with risk scoring, narrative generation, and 30-day filing deadline tracking.
